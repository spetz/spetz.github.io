I'm speaking at [P99 CONF 2026](https://p99conf.io/), the online conference about low latency and high performance engineering, on October 21-22.

The talk is an engineering playbook told through [Apache Iggy](https://iggy.apache.org/): thread-per-core, shared-nothing, io_uring, zero-copy and Viewstamped Replication, and how these choices keep tail latency low and predictable at high throughput.
