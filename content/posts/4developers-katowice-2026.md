I'm speaking at [4Developers Katowice 2026](https://4developers.org.pl/lecture-katowice-2026/#id=107884) on October 8.

The talk is an engineering playbook told through [Apache Iggy](https://iggy.apache.org/): thread-per-core, shared-nothing, io_uring, zero-copy and Viewstamped Replication. Where the milliseconds hide in a message streaming platform, and what it takes to get them back.
